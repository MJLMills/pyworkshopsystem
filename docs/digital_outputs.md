## Digital Outputs

The Music Thing Modular Workshop System Computer has several ditigal outputs:

* 6 LEDs arranged in a 2x3 matrix
* Two pulse output sockets

As digital outputs that can only provide values of on and off, these are limited in their general usage. They can be 
turned on, turned off, toggled (switched to the state they are currently _not_ in) and pulsed (turned on for a period
of time and then turned off).
